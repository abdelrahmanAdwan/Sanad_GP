## Question
Do annotators play each segment while labeling, or read the transcript only?

## Notes
Watching means labels reflect what was said, not ASR mistakes (ViClaim's tool showed the video beside the transcript). It is slower and needs a tool that plays each segment. Guidelines v0 assumes annotators watch.
