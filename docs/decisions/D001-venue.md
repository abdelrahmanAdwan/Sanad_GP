## Question
Which venue does the semester-1 dataset paper target?

## Decision
The ICWSM 2027 dataset track is **not the only target**. Other venues are allowed.

## Consequences
- Proposal §3 and §6.7 name ICWSM 2027 as the target; they now need a sentence about alternatives.
- The same paper cannot be under review at two venues at once (dual submission is not allowed).
- Until the follow-up is answered, plan against 15 Jan 2027 so that no option is lost.
