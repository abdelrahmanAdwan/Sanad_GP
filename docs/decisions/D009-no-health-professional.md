## Decision
There is no health professional for semester 2. The evaluation will rely on information in trusted medical sources.

## Consequences
Changes proposal §3 (objective 5), §4 (assumptions) and Table 8 ("300 Arabic claims judged by a health professional").

## Replacement options (suggestions, not decided)
- **A.** Use claims already checked by professional fact-checkers (health subset of AraFacts) as an external gold standard.
- **B.** Team members judge, double-annotated with agreement reported, whether the evidence base supports the claim (a defined question that needs no medical training), rather than whether the claim is medically true.
- **C.** If a health professional becomes available later, they review a small sample (for example 50 claims).
