## Question
In which order are the five topics (diabetes, hypertension, nutrition and diets, herbal and alternative medicine, cancer) collected and annotated?

## Suggestion (not decided)
Start with diabetes: published studies document widespread misinformation in Arabic diabetes videos (Gharbi et al., 2023; Almogbel et al., 2021), so claim-rich videos should be easy to find.

## Evidence collected 2026-11-10
Per-topic summary of published Arabic YouTube studies, with what each implies for the order, is in `docs/collection-protocol.md` §1. Short version: diabetes and cancer are well documented (Gharbi 2023; Ayoub 2021; Abu Daabes & Kharbat 2019); herbal medicine is very claim-dense and overlaps cancer (Kharbat & Abu Daabes 2023); nutrition has only a diabetes-diet study (Dabbus 2025); no Arabic-specific hypertension study was found. Suggested order: diabetes → herbal/alternative → cancer → nutrition/diets → hypertension (hypertension last as the most different held-out topic for RQ2). Alternative: diabetes → hypertension → nutrition → herbal → cancer.
